# salmon
Preheat oven to 350°F–400°F. Place fillets skin-side down on a lined baking sheet. Bake for 12–15 minutes (or until the fish flakes easily with a fork).

The "Pat Dry" Step: Before adding any oil or spray, always pat the salmon fillet very dry with a paper towel. Moisture on the surface creates steam, which prevents the fish from browning properly.
The Oil: A light drizzle of olive oil, avocado oil, or melted butter works perfectly. Use a brush or your fingers to coat the top (and sides) of the fish evenly
